One real chain from the day: a corridor goes quiet and its lights go off; a badge and a phone report the same person.
A rule engine on an MQTT-style bus over a 1-second simulation. No model, no embedding, no vector store.

Output

lights and AC per room, attendance, tailgate alerts, a roll-call
