Lahore Twin · Hospital github.com/hammasbuilds/lahore-twin

Real records from one day: a patient's way through the emergency department, and a monitor alarm under Connected.
Agents (generator coroutines) on a shared task board, rules on an MQTT-style bus. No model, no embedding, no vector store.

Input

a day of emergency arrivals, a 36-bed ward, alarms, families

Output

waits, alarms answered, equipment found, beds turned round
